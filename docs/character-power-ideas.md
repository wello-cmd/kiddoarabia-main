# Kiddo character power proposals

These are fictional story ideas for review, not product or nutrition claims. Character designs remain unchanged.

| Character | Flavor link | Proposed power |
|---|---|---|
| Pops | Choco Pops | Cocoa Bounce: leaps between bouncing chocolate spheres. |
| King | Choco Flakes | Crunch Shield: stacks cocoa flakes into a protective wall. |
| Loopy | Fruit Rings | Rainbow Portals: connects places with colorful ring gateways. |
| Bana | Banana Pillow | Banana Glide: slides through the air on banana-shaped paths. |
| Buzz | Honey Rings | Honey Trail: draws glowing honey paths for friends to follow. |
| Berry | Strawberry Pillow | Berry Bloom: brings a fictional strawberry garden to life. |
| Pillow | Crunchy Pillow | Pillow Cloud: creates soft resting clouds and bouncy landing pads. |
| Creamo | Choco Creamo | Cream Swirl: spins a cookie-and-cream whirlwind to move obstacles. |
| Byte | Choco Rice | Cocoa Sparks: sends playful pixel-like cocoa sparks through puzzles. |
| Flaky | Corn Flakes | Golden Gust: rides a wind of golden corn flakes. |
| Scoops | Cocoa Scoops | Scoop Orbit: moves cocoa scoops around like little planets. |

## Future illustrated adventure
