## 2026-06-30 - Replace Blocking Native Alerts with Toast Notifications\n**Learning:** Using native `alert()` for form submissions or simple confirmations degrades user experience because it blocks the main thread, halting interactions and scripts until dismissed.\n**Action:** Use the existing `sonner` library (`toast.success()`) to provide non-blocking, modern micro-UX feedback for successful interactions like subscribing to a newsletter or submitting a contact form.
