## 2026-06-10 - Adding Accessible Click Handlers to Recipes\n**Learning:** When adding interactive click handlers to elements inside other interactive elements (e.g. recipe cards), we need `e.stopPropagation()` to prevent unintended navigation, and non-navigational buttons require `aria-label` and should ideally have visual feedback (e.g. via toast) to avoid silently eating clicks.\n**Action:** Add `e.stopPropagation()`, descriptive `aria-label`, and `toast()` feedback to all inner interactive elements.
